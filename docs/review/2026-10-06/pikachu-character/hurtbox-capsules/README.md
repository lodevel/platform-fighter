# Optional posed 2D capsule hurtboxes

This engine addition accepts an explicit body-relative capsule on a hurtbox. Existing rectangular hurtboxes retain their behavior. It does not derive a capsule from a drawing, native bone declaration or movement collider, and no Pikachu native posed hurtboxes are enabled.

The capsule's endpoints already include the authored pose relative to the movement-body centre. Defender facing mirrors their x coordinates. `offsetX`, `offsetY`, `width` and `height` remain in older saves/tools; when an explicit capsule is supplied they do not substitute its collision geometry or apply a second offset. A derived enclosure is used only to find contact candidates.

Attack-capsule and legacy rectangular contacts use the rounded shape before region priority, damage scaling, intangibility, sweet spots or contact confirmation. The live candidate scan still finds limbs extending outside the movement body. Projectile rectangles use an analytical continuous sweep against a rounded convex Minkowski sum, preserving earliest contact and reflection ordering without sampled positions or enclosure-corner hits. F3 draws actual green capsule outlines and mirrors legacy rectangle offsets with defender facing. Existing red attack rectangles still display their Matter sensor enclosure; this change does not convert that separate attack overlay into rounded source-region drawing.

[Checks and implementation identities](checks.json) record61 focused tests: pure geometry7, actual Matter contact10, save/editor/factory6, F3 formatting/drawing19 and continuous projectile sweeps19. Another330 existing source, contact, repeat-clock, landing, throw and schema checks pass. Save/load includes posed endpoints, radius, intangibility and damage multiplier; malformed capsules fail instead of falling back to rectangles. Resetting a saved fixture fighter clears its temporary capsule with its action.

[Production browser validation](browser/report.json) passes34 cases in both facings, with exact served bundle hash and live engine identity. [Right-facing](browser/capsule-facing-1.png) and [left-facing](browser/capsule-facing--1.png) F3 screenshots were inspected: the green rotated capsule sits beside the existing green body rectangle and mirrors with its owner. The enclosing rectangle is not used as its final contact shape. [Shared production regressions](regression-summary.json) also pass on that exact served bundle:42 DK collision cases,94 DK move scenarios/227 checks,4 captured-target throw scenarios,8 fair cases,38 landing cases and3 DK Lab save/reload checks. [Build identity](build.json) records the separate output; workspace `dist`, the first jab snapshot and the rough tilt preview are unchanged. The browser harness uses deliberately synthetic authored limbs and DK fixture bodies. It must not be interpreted as native Pikachu vulnerability, animation or roster acceptance. The existing melee multiplier helper still clamps its maximum to at least1; this change verifies1.5 amplification and does not claim new below1 damage-reduction support. [Fixture corrections](harness-corrections.json) record startup/index/multiplier setup fixes without changing the production bundle. Separate thrown-item/body-impact and grab-capture policies are outside this attack/projectile contact change.

The captured [native12 capsule declarations](../sources/native-hurtbox-capsules.json) and [primary consumer evidence](../sources/hurtbox-consumer-contract.md) remain separate. Native posed matrices, model/motion scaling and status precedence have not been obtained. No renderer-generated bone positions or guessed native projection are adopted.
