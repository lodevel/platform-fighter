Provider selection does not end a generation deferral. During deferral prepare
the references, guides and instructions without calling a generator or asking
again for credentials. A handoff is planning data until runtime consumes it.
Claim handoff completion only when every requested pose, necessary source-frame
mapping (or explicitly chosen authored adaptation), anchor, branch and required
integration task is specified. Essential pending motion review means partial
preparation. Future provider execution settings may remain deferred when its
capabilities are not yet known.
